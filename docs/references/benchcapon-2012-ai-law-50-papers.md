# Bench-Capon 2012 — A History of AI and Law in 50 Papers

**Citation.** Bench-Capon, T., Araszkiewicz, M., Ashley, K., et al. (2012). A history of AI and Law in 50
papers: 25 years of the international conference on AI and Law. *Artificial Intelligence and Law*, 20(3),
215–319.
**Link.** https://doi.org/10.1007/s10506-012-9131-x · **Type.** classic (field retrospective) · **Topic.** AI & Law

## Why it matters to our work
The **target journal's own** retrospective of the field — essential for framing our submission within *Artificial
Intelligence and Law*. It maps the discipline (legal reasoning, argumentation, case-based reasoning, and the
later turn toward **data-driven / empirical legal analytics**), letting us position a *process-mining*
contribution as part of the field's data-driven, transparency-oriented strand rather than its symbolic-reasoning
core.

## Key takeaways
- Defines what counts as AI & Law scholarship and its evolution — the scoping argument for our paper's fit.
- Complement with *"Thirty years of AI and law: the third decade"* (AIL, 2022,
  https://doi.org/10.1007/s10506-022-09327-6) for the most recent, ML/empirical decade.

## How we cite it
Introduction / journal-fit framing: situating administrative-process mining within the AI & Law agenda.
