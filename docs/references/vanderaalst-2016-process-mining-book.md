# van der Aalst 2016 — Process Mining: Data Science in Action

**Citation.** van der Aalst, W. M. P. (2016). *Process Mining: Data Science in Action* (2nd ed.). Springer.
**Link.** https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-662-49851-4 · **Type.** classic (textbook) · **Topic.** process-mining foundations

## Why it matters to our work
The definitive reference for the entire field our project sits in — event logs, process discovery,
conformance checking, and the control-flow/time/organizational perspectives. Everything we do (turning the SEI
activity log into a `SEGUIDA_POR` graph, discovering process variants, comparing observed vs modeled flow) is an
instance of the process-mining program this book defines.

## Key takeaways
- Process mining bridges data mining and BPM: *discovery* (model from log), *conformance* (log vs model), and
  *enhancement*.
- The directly-follows relation, trace, and case notions we use throughout come from here.
- Frames process mining as a transparency/audit instrument — the bridge to our AI & Law framing.

## How we cite it
Related work / foundations: the canonical definition of process mining and its tasks; justification that an
event log of administrative activities is a legitimate, auditable object of study.
