# COVID-19 — pandemic-driven digitalization of Brazilian public administration

**Citation.** *Digital Transformation Initiatives in Public Administration During the COVID-19 Pandemic in
Brazil: Unveiling Challenges and Opportunities* (2021). Springer.
**Link.** https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-84789-0_2 · **Type.** recent · Brazilian · **Topic.** e-government · digital transformation

## Why it matters to our work — *why the SEI is the way it is*
**The context that explains our data's noise.** The Piauí-government SEI was **rolled out during the COVID-19
pandemic** and **its use is still maturing**. The literature documents that the pandemic *accelerated* public-
sector digitalization but the **rushed deployment revealed gaps** in standardization, training, and digital
literacy — exactly why our log shows unstandardized actions, inconsistent routing, and weak enforcement. This is
not a defect of our analysis but a **property of an emerging, maturing system**, which:

- justifies treating standardization as *low* (and modeling the **as-is**, not an idealized to-be);
- frames the temporal angle as **process maturation / concept drift** (usage stabilizing over time);
- positions our method as a **maturity instrument**: data-driven, rule-valid models that help an immature SEI
  deployment converge toward consistency — a transparency *and* improvement tool.

## Key takeaways
- COVID-19 accelerated e-gov adoption in developing countries; rapid deployment ⇒ standardization/maturity gaps.
