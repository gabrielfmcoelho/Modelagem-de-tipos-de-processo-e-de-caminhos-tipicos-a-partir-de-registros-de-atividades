# Dumas 2018 — Fundamentals of Business Process Management

**Citation.** Dumas, M., La Rosa, M., Mendling, J., & Reijers, H. A. (2018). *Fundamentals of Business Process
Management* (2nd ed.). Springer.
**Link.** https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-662-56509-4 · **Type.** classic (textbook) · **Topic.** BPM lifecycle · process redesign

## Why it matters to our work — the *objective*
The reference for our stated goal: **giving managers a baseline to rethink and redesign processes.** Many
managers are unaware of which process types exist or how they actually flow; this textbook formalizes the **BPM
lifecycle** — *identification → discovery → analysis → **redesign** → implementation → monitoring* — into which
our contribution slots precisely: we automate **discovery** (clustering + as-is timelines) and **analysis**
(conformance/validity, bottlenecks) at scale, producing the **as-is baseline** that the redesign phase requires.

It also frames the value proposition for an immature, unstandardized SEI: process mining is the evidence base
that turns process improvement from opinion-driven to data-driven — exactly the "transparency → digital
transformation → redesign" arc we pursue.

## Key takeaways
- The BPM lifecycle; the as-is model as the prerequisite for principled redesign.
