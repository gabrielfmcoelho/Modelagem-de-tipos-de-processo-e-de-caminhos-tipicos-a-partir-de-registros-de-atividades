# Guidotti 2018 — A Survey of Methods for Explaining Black Box Models (XAI)

**Citation.** Guidotti, R., Monreale, A., Ruggieri, S., Turini, F., Giannotti, F., & Pedreschi, D. (2018). A
Survey of Methods for Explaining Black Box Models. *ACM Computing Surveys*, 51(5), 1–42.
**Link.** https://doi.org/10.1145/3236009 · **Type.** classic (XAI survey) · **Topic.** explainable AI

## Why it matters to our work
Explainability is the **AI & Law journal's core demand** of any administrative-decision system. This survey gives
the vocabulary (intrinsic vs post-hoc, global vs local, model-agnostic vs model-specific) to position our outputs
as **intrinsically interpretable**: a chronological swimlane of a representative process *is* a human-readable
explanation of "how this process type runs", the Transformer's **attention scores** are post-hoc explanations of
a prediction, and the **constrained-validity rules** are explicit, auditable constraints. Our artifacts are
transparency instruments, not black boxes.

## Key takeaways
- The accuracy-vs-interpretability trade-off and the taxonomy of explanation methods.
