# Council of Europe 2022 — Artificial Intelligence and Administrative Law

**Citation.** Council of Europe, European Committee on Legal Co-operation (CDCJ) (2022). *Artificial
Intelligence and Administrative Law*. CDCJ(2022)31E.
**Link.** https://www.coe.int/documents/22298481/0/CDCJ(2022)31E+-+FINAL+6.pdf · **Type.** recent (policy/legal report) · **Topic.** AI & administrative law

## Why it matters to our work
A current, authoritative legal-policy treatment of **AI in administrative procedure** — the precise legal
context of our work (SEI is an administrative-process system). It articulates the requirements our method serves:
**transparency, the duty to give reasons, predictability, legality, and accountability** of automated/assisted
administrative action. A *valid, explainable* model of how an administrative process actually runs is an
instrument for exactly these duties.

## Key takeaways
- Names transparency/accountability/legality as the legal constraints on AI in administration.
- Notes the tension between automation efficiency and rule-of-law guarantees — which our **rule-constrained
  generation** (provably valid traces) directly addresses.

## How we cite it
Framing / motivation: the legal duties (transparency, reason-giving, accountability) our auditable process
models support; the AI & Law angle that an *Artificial Intelligence and Law* reviewer expects.
