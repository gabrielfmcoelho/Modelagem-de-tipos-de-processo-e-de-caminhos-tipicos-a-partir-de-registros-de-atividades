# Process mining in public administration / e-government

**Theme file** (several cites — the e-gov application domain).

## Representative references
- **Public procurement (EU):** *Leveraging process mining and event-log enrichment in European public
  procurement analysis* (ScienceDirect, 2025) — multinational TED dataset; improves **compliance monitoring**,
  control-flow and timeliness. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2212473X25000173
- **Procurement conformance (Philippines):** process discovery on procurement data + **conformance with the
  procurement law** + problematic-node detection. (Springer, *Process Mining on a Public Procurement Dataset*,
  2024) https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-74630-7_35
- **Transparency (Brazil):** *Transparency promoted by process mining: an exploratory study in a public health
  product management process* — process mining as a **transparency instrument** in a Brazilian public body.

## Why it matters to our work
Establishes the **e-government / public-administration** application area and its recurring goals — **legal
compliance, transparency, efficiency, rework reduction** — which are exactly our objectives and the *Artificial
Intelligence and Law* journal's concerns. These papers show process mining is an accepted, published method for
auditing public processes against the law; our work extends it with unsupervised clustering + ML generation.

## How we cite it
Application domain + motivation (transparency/compliance in public administration); evidence that auditing
administrative processes against legal/ideal models is an established research line.
